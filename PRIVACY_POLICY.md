# Privacy Policy for MYPA

**Last Updated: February 1, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

MYPA ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise handle your information when you use our mobile application (the "App") and related services (the "Services").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our practices, please do not use the App or Services.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information**: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password.
- **Profile Information**: You may optionally provide a profile picture, bio, and other personal details to customize your experience.
- **Tasks and Goals**: Information about tasks, goals, challenges, and focus sessions you create.
- **Communication**: Messages, posts, comments, and other content you share within circles or with other users.
- **Payment Information**: If you purchase premium features, we collect payment information through secure third-party payment processors.
- **Feedback**: Any feedback, suggestions, or support inquiries you submit.

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information

- **Device Information**: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
- **Usage Data**: How you interact with the App, including features used, time spent, and actions taken.
- **Log Data**: IP address, access times, referring URL, pages visited, and error information.
- **Location Information**: If you grant permission, we may collect approximate location based on IP address or GPS.
- **Cookies and Tracking**: We use cookies, analytics trackers, and similar technologies to understand App usage.

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

- **Social Authentication**: If you log in via social media platforms, we receive your profile information from those services.
- **Contacts**: If you grant permission, we may access your device contacts to help you find friends in the App.
- **Calendar**: If you grant permission, we may access your calendar to help coordinate focus sessions.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

- **Service Delivery**: To create and maintain your account, provide features, and deliver the Services.
- **Personalization**: To customize your experience and provide tailored recommendations.
- **Communication**: To send transactional emails, push notifications, and respond to your inquiries.
- **Analytics**: To analyze usage patterns, improve the App, and develop new features.
- **Safety & Security**: To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents.
- **Legal Compliance**: To comply with applicable laws and regulations.
- **Marketing**: To send promotional content (only with your consent).

## 4. How We Share Your Information

### 4.1 Within Circles

Information you share in circles is visible to circle members according to your privacy settings:
- Public profiles and shared content are visible to all circle members
- Private messages are only visible to intended recipients
- You control what information is shared in each circle

### 4.2 Service Providers

We may share information with third-party service providers who assist with:
- Cloud infrastructure and storage
- Payment processing
- Analytics and tracking
- Customer support
- Email delivery

These providers are contractually obligated to use your information only for providing services to us.

### 4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority, or to protect our legal rights and the safety of our users.

### 4.4 Business Transfers

If we merge, acquire, or sell assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change and provide you with options regarding your information.

### 4.5 With Your Consent

We may share information with third parties when you explicitly consent to such sharing.

## 5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide the Services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

When you delete your account:
- Your account information is removed from our systems
- Your posts and messages may remain in circles at the discretion of other members
- Aggregated analytics data may be retained

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your information:

### 7.1 California Residents (CCPA)

- Right to know what personal information we collect
- Right to access your personal information
- Right to delete your personal information
- Right to opt-out of the sale of your personal information
- Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

### 7.2 EU/UK Residents (GDPR/UK-GDPR)

- Right to access your personal data
- Right to rectification of inaccurate data
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing
- Right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority

### 7.3 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at: privacy@mypa.app

We will respond to your request within 30 days (or as required by law).

If we become aware that we have collected information from a child without verifiable parental consent, we will delete such information immediately.
